Artificial intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science focused on creating systems capable of performing tasks that typically require human intelligence. These tasks include understanding natural language, recognizing patterns, learning from data, making decisions, and solving complex problems. Advances in computing power and the availability of massive datasets have accelerated the development of machine learning and deep learning models over the past decade.

Today, AI is used in a wide variety of industries. In healthcare, it helps doctors detect diseases earlier by analyzing medical images and patient records. In finance, AI models are used to identify fraudulent transactions, assess risk, and predict market trends. Educational platforms leverage AI to personalize learning experiences, while manufacturers use intelligent systems to optimize production processes and reduce operational costs.

Although AI offers many benefits, it also introduces important challenges. Concerns about privacy, algorithmic bias, transparency, and the future of work have become central topics of discussion among researchers, policymakers, and technology companies. As a result, there is a growing emphasis on developing responsible AI systems that are fair, secure, explainable, and aligned with ethical principles.
